Welcome to MarkV

A quiet place to read markdown. Open a file, settle in, and take it in at your own pace.

  • Stays on this device
  • No account
  • Works offline

Open something of yours

Drop a .md file anywhere on this page, or use Files at the top left. Open a whole folder and every note in it is a click away.

Drop a markdown file to start reading

.md, .markdown or .txt. Nothing leaves your device.

Make it yours

Press Aa at the bottom to change the text size, line spacing, width and palette. MarkV remembers your choices, so every document opens the way you like it.

Find your way

Longer documents get an outline on the right that follows along as you read. Press ⌘K to search your files and run any command.

When you want to write, switch to Edit. Coming back to Read returns you to the same spot.

See the shape of things

Diagrams, tables, maths and code all render the way you would expect.

Ask, if you want to

Connect your own AI key (Groq, OpenRouter, or Ollama on your computer) to ask questions about a document or map its ideas. It is entirely optional, and your key is stored only in this browser.

Ready when you are

Your first file is one drop away.
