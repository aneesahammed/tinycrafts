Toolbar: three directions

Today: 14 equally weighted bordered buttons wrap onto two rows and take about 140px off the reading surface. Reload and Auto sit disabled most of the time, ⌘K is a button that only shows a hint, and the destructive Clear lives in primary chrome. Each direction below keeps every feature, fits on one 52px row, and gives the bar a clear hierarchy. Shortcuts shown in menus are illustrative.

A

Quiet bar

Three zones. Left is the document (title, words, a Live chip only when a file handle is watched). Center is one search field that absorbs Search and the ⌘K hint. Right is a Read/Edit segment, four borderless reading icons, Share as the single labeled action, and a ⋯ menu for everything occasional.

B

Labeled menus

A document app pattern (Docs, Notion, iA Writer). Two text menus, File and View, hold every secondary action with its shortcut, so nothing is a mystery icon. The right side keeps only the three things people do constantly: switch mode, share, export.

C

Reader dock

Splits chrome by intent, matching markv’s calm-reading purpose. The top bar is about the document (title, search, Edit, Share, ⋯). Reading tools move to a small floating dock under the text that fades out while you scroll down and returns on scroll up or pointer move.
