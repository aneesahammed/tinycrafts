Inline pills — segmented expansion

Click Export. Two pill buttons slide in beside it: PDF and MD. Click a pill — export starts immediately. Pills retract after. No floating menu at all.

Wins No menu floating over content. The choices ARE the toolbar — you see and act at the same level. Animation feels alive.

Cost Toolbar width fluctuates on expand. On crowded toolbars this can push items into the overflow sheet. Best when toolbar already has slack.

My pick: B · Split button

Of the four alternatives, the split button does the most design work for the least visual cost. The label itself carries the format ("Export PDF") so users know what's about to happen — no opening required. The caret is the explicit switch. Default-remembers-last-choice means most exports are one click for repeat users.

If you want the safest move with the least state to manage, A (refined menu) is the next-best choice — same interaction shape as current, just better proportions and typography.

  • B · Split button. Most refined. Two-click flexibility, one-click muscle memory.
  • A · Refined menu. Lowest risk. Cleans up current without changing how it works.
  • D · Inline pills. Most distinctive. Best if toolbar has room.
  • C · Icon-only. Cleanest, but loses discoverability for new users.
