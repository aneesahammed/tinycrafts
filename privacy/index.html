Privacy

What leaves your browser

TinyCrafts has no accounts, no database and no server of its own. Files you open in these tools are processed in your browser. The few things that do leave it are listed here, tool by tool.

Updated
October 29, 2026
Contact
support@tinycrafts.ai

Analytics

The home page, markV and DataDuck count page views with GoatCounter. No cookies, no personal data.

markV tinycrafts.ai/markv

Stays in your browser

  • Your Markdown files and document library.
  • Your Groq API key, if you add one.

Leaves your browser

  • Q&A with Groq, if you turn it on: your question and the relevant excerpts of the document go to Groq, using your key.

DataDuck tinycrafts.ai/dataduck

Stays in your browser

  • The CSV and Parquet files you open. DuckDB-WASM reads them in your browser; they are never uploaded.
  • Every SQL query and its results.
  • Your Claude or Groq API key, if you save it (encrypted in this browser).

Leaves your browser

  • Ask DataDuck, if you turn it on: your question, the file name, and column names, types and summary stats go to Anthropic or Groq, using your key.
  • Result rows are sent only when you click Send aggregate rows for AI summary, capped at 50 rows.

SeeQLite tinycrafts.ai/seeqlite

Stays in your browser

  • The SQLite file you open, its schema, every query and its results.

Leaves your browser

  • Nothing. SeeQLite has no analytics.

Pagecrumb Chrome extension

Pagecrumb turns the page you're on into Markdown locally in Chrome. It has no server and no analytics, and it reads a page only after you click it. Its permissions and details are in Pagecrumb's privacy policy.

Products on their own sites

Sentinel, ShotBar and Chronicle live on their own subdomains and are not covered by this page.

What TinyCrafts doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups.
  • No ads and no tracking cookies.
  • No TinyCrafts server ever receives your files.
  • Your data is never sold or shared.

Contact and changes

Questions, bugs or security reports: support@tinycrafts.ai. When this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
